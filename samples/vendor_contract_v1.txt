MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (VENDOR VERSION A - STANDARD)

1. SERVICE SCOPE
Vendor shall provide Cloud Infrastructure Operations and Maintenance services as outlined in Statement of Work #1.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments shall incur a interest charge of 1.5% per month.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party's total liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid by Client to Vendor in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Vendor guarantees 99.9% uptime for core infrastructure services. In the event of an SLA breach, Client is entitled to a 10% invoice credit for the affected month.

5. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.
